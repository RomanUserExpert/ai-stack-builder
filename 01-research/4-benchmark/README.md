# Stage 4 — Benchmark

A scoring rubric built from what stages 1–3 found, applied to the best product in the world at each
of our four core flows. It exists to make stage 5 decidable: without a rubric *"which of these five
is best"* is settled by taste.

**Start with** [`benchmark.md`](benchmark.md) — the matrix, the rules the scoring follows, the
argument behind every score, and eight findings. **Then read its finalisation section**, which says
what the rubric does *not* measure.
