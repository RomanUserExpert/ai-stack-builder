# Lesson 01 — Research and benchmark

**Research stages 1–5, signed off on 2026-09-02.** What vendors sell, what the key flows look like
elsewhere, what actually breaks, how the best of them score, and which shape our key flow takes. The
page for the lesson is [`research.html`](research.html), phase 01 of the course.

**Start with [`FINAL.md`](FINAL.md)** — the closing document: what the phase produced, what it decided,
and how the six open questions were settled. [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) is the spec; this folder is
the evidence under it.

| | Stage | Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Landscape | [`1-landscape/`](1-landscape/README.md) | 15 competitors in three groups, compared on five axes, plus the nearest dead competitor read from source |
| 2 | Flows | [`2-flows/`](2-flows/README.md) | Twelve mechanisms captured from live products, with notes per capture |
| 3 | Pain | [`3-pain/`](3-pain/README.md) | What actually breaks, read from public issue trackers — and what that instrument cannot see |
| 4 | Benchmark | [`4-benchmark/`](4-benchmark/README.md) | 15 product-and-flow cells scored on five categories |
| 5 | Patterns | [`5-patterns/`](5-patterns/README.md) | Five shapes for the key flow; the chosen one is in `CLAUDE.md` §8 |

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`FINAL.md`](FINAL.md) | The close. **Read first.** |
| [`research-plan.md`](research-plan.md) | The spine, ending in **the register** — every open question, from every lesson, with an ID. The only list of open questions in the repo. |
| [`research.md`](research.md) | The consolidated read in five sections. **A digest, never a source of truth.** |
| [`research.html`](research.html) | The same research as one self-contained page, 34 captures embedded. Generated — see [`../tools/`](../tools/README.md). |

**The register lives here and is used by every lesson.** It started as the research phase's list of
open questions and outlived it; lessons 02–05 add their entries to the same file.

Lesson 02 — personas and jobs to be done, stages 6 and 7 — is [`../02-personas-jtbd/`](../02-personas-jtbd/README.md).
