Phase 07 · UI assembly

Every screen, built from one kit

Phase 7 of 12 · all seven of the phase’s stages, one skipped by the owner · 19–23 Oct 2026 · source of truth: kit.css for the look, tokens.css for the values, microcopy.md for the words

Phase 06 found the look and proved it on the main job. This phase turns it into the product. Painting the rest of the screens one by one, “like the sample”, would have let the look drift a little on every page. So a kit stands between the style and the screens — one stylesheet, one shell, one showcase — and every screen is generated from its wireframe on that kit and nothing else. A build that finds a style of its own on any page fails.

START

Overview

Five days, every screen of the product dressed, and the two lists they produced.

What we saw

  • A screen is a composition, not a painting. The main job had been painted by hand in phase 06, and the first read of it found the same component drawn three ways — a page head, an empty-state tile, a finding box. Sixty more screens painted the same way would have multiplied that.
  • The wireframes are the specification, and they already held the answer. Structure and words were settled in phases 04 and 05; a dressed screen that changed either would have been a second specification. So the twins keep the wireframe’s markup and lines exactly, and the audit proves it: 0 pages whose words differ from their wireframe.
  • The kit was read, not drawn. The inventory came from all the wireframe pages and the sitemap — 41 components, each named with the screens it stands on — and the kit was lifted from the painted screens, so nothing in it is remembered or invented.
  • The worst defect of the phase was ours, and only walking every link found it. The shell was swapped into the flow screens and broke 402 links; a review that rendered every step and followed every link caught it, and a guard now refuses it.
  • Measuring keeps finding what looking does not. The new contrast pairs caught a focus ring at 1.21:1 in the light theme; the audit caught a file name on lime at 1.2:1 inside a button — both invisible on a quick look.

What we decided

  • One stylesheet, kit.css, and every screen links only it. Its :root mirrors tokens.css character for character; its classes hold no literal value.
  • The wireframes stay grey, and the design is their generated twin — the owner’s call from phase 06, kept. A mockup is never edited by hand.
  • One way in for every kind of change — a value, a look, a structure, a word — and nine guards that fail the build when a change goes in anywhere else.
  • One primary per screen, settled by the build, with a fixed rank from an open dialog down to the Export stage.
  • Our own failure is neutral, not Problem rose — and so are the keys reminder and the blast radius, because they are disclosures, not findings.
  • Оставляем means the kit. When the owner keeps a visual change, it goes into the kit, is signed in DESIGN.md, and reaches every screen where the component stands.

STAGE 01

DESIGN.md, read off the painted screens

The look written down from what was drawn, not from what was meant — and where the two disagreed, the screens won.

/impeccable document read the 22 painted mockups and tokens.css. It asked for a north star and a character for the components, as it always does; the answers were taken from what already existed — The Inspector’s Report, from the voice’s first principle, and quiet and exact — so a new metaphor could not pull the look somewhere else. DESIGN.md sits at the root, describes and points, and holds no value of its own: its frontmatter mirrors tokens.css, and a comment says which file wins.

concept.md was corrected to follow the mockups, each line marked Phase 07: the density, the current place, the cut-out’s inward shadow, the radius on cards and dialogs — and four things drawn but never written, among them one primary per screen and the Skipped stage. Two conflicts were named for the owner rather than written in: glass was nearly absent, and the active row seemed to carry a ring. The owner kept glass as it is, and the ring turned out to be the cut-out’s own edge, one step brighter.

STAGE 03

The kit: a stylesheet, a shell, a showcase

Three files, and every screen is built from the first.

FileWhat it is
ui/kit.css110 variables in one flat :root, copied from tokens.css by the build, and 177 classes with no literal value in them. Generated by tools/build_kit.py
ui/shell.htmlThe shell as markup, in its three variants. A screen takes its header from here rather than drawing its own
ui/kit.htmlThe showcase: every component in every state it has, cut from the real pages rather than typed, with the product’s own lines — a word on it that is not in microcopy.md fails the build

Flat on purpose. One level of variables; the split into primitives, semantic and component tokens is phase 08, and it is not anticipated here. And it was proved before it was used: the 22 painted mockups were shot, moved onto kit.css alone, and shot again — 22 of 22 identical to the pixel. The same test now runs on every page after every change, and a painted screen may move only when the change meant it to.

STAGE 04–05

Assembly: every screen, through one door

How a screen is made, where each kind of change goes in, and what stops a change that goes in anywhere else.

Stage 04, the product’s own visuals, was skipped by the owner: the product has one photo, the sharer’s portrait, not a layer of imagery, and the stock portrait is recorded as accepted rather than as a defect. Assembly went by the viewer’s groups — the configuring mode and the detached row first, under the owner’s eye, because they close the main job — then the door, the libraries and the Item sheet, every Run, the receiver’s pages, and every flow.

A tone the structure cannot carry — danger, warning, the one primary, a state class, mono for machine text — is set by the build’s transform, never by a class added to the wireframe only for looks. And every state is a class, never a guess from the content.

Nine guards, and they stop the build

  1. No value outside a variable in the kit’s classes
  2. No style on a screen — no style=, no <style>
  3. Every class is the kit’s, save a short named list
  4. Every variable is declared, in the kit and in its showcase
  5. The kit mirrors the tokens, character for character
  6. One stylesheet: a page links kit.css and nothing else
  7. One primary on any screen
  8. The words are the product’s: the showcase against microcopy.md
  9. Coverage: 83 of 83 pages and 241 of 241 flow steps have a twin

Stages 06–07 — conclusion

Every fix went in through the same door as everything else, so it reached every screen at once. A markup fix made once in the generator is the advantage of twins: nothing had to be carried by hand to forty pages, and nothing could be missed on the forty-first.
