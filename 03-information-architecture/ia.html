START

How to read this

Every screen here exists because a job needs it. A screen with no job and a job with no screen are the same defect, and both are counted at the end.

This page is derived, not composed. The tree, the diagrams and the matrix below are read out of sitemap.md and flows.md when the page is built, so it cannot drift from the work. Nothing was redrawn for the page.

Two boundaries hold everywhere on it. No appearance — no colour, no type scale, no component named in a way that implies a look; that starts at phase 06. And, since 20 Sep 2026, no backend: the product is online and what the server is stays undecided, so every rule is phrased as what a person sees and what a surface may claim. Two decisions taken that afternoon were phrased as guarantees about storage and were restated the same evening. Neither changed what it decided.

Three kinds of ending

The flows end in three ways, and the third was added on 20 Sep when applying one question — is this person actually stuck? — moved four endings out of red.

Done

The job closed. The person got what they came for.

Cost

The job did not close, and nothing stopped them. They proceeded, knowingly or not, and paid for it. A cost you were told about is not an obstruction.

Stuck

The person cannot proceed. There is no action left that the product offers. Three of these survive in eight diagrams — two are outside our reach, and one is the product’s only refusal.

Two flows have no Stuck at all, and that is a result rather than a gap. They are the flows about findings, and a finding never blocks anybody: the specification says export is never disabled, so a problem you cannot fix from its row is confirmed and shipped, not refused. Until 20 Sep one of those two ended in a wall, which contradicted the specification and the diagram beside it.

Seven screens and one orphan, plus four nodes that are not screens — the Item add/edit overlay, the configuring mode, the library panel inside it, and the detached row. Six screens until 20 Sep, when the product turned out to be online and a door appeared in front of everything.

Group 0 is not a fourth situation and is marked so it cannot be read as one. Nobody has a situation called I would like to sign in. The door exists because the product has a server, and every other node in the tree answers to something somebody wants. The receiver never passes through it: a shared link is opened anonymously, and the branch where they take the archive stays free of any door — which is what makes it the branch that must always work. The only way they meet it is by choosing to keep a copy, and at that moment they have stopped being a receiver.

STEP 02b

What each thing is

A tree is a hierarchy. This asks what each node is, on one test: could somebody be sent there and arrive?

KindTest it passesTest it fails
PlaceReload lands you here · Back is meaningful · the address survives—
ModeTakes the surface, has a way outNot addressable — a reload cannot restore it
OverlaySummoned over what you were doing, dismissed back onto itNever in the address; loses nothing when dismissed
RegionOn the screen whenever its place isFails all three of the others — it is part of a place
StateA place or a mode looking different because of the dataNot navigated to at all

Six places, and that is the whole navigable surface

Library with one address per scope · Projects · Project · the two the shared link creates, Shared project and Shared item · and Sign in. Everything else is a mode, an overlay, a region, a state or content.

The count has moved in both directions, which is the point of having a test rather than a list. It was six, fell to five when the owner kept the item as a form rather than a place, and returned to six when the sign-in passed all three tests. The biggest mechanical change the builder has had — a command palette replaced by a library panel — moved it by nothing at all, because a panel that is on screen whenever its place is fails place, mode and overlay in turn, and what is left is a region.

The temptation was to invent a fourth kind for the door — place, mode, overlay, gate — and it was refused. A classification that grows a category whenever something does not fit has stopped classifying. What is true instead is worth saying plainly: the sign-in is the only one of the six that exists for the product rather than for the person’s work.

STEP 03

Navigation and depth

Which places a person may reach without being sent, and how far the main job sits from the first screen.

Two global entries, where the method asks for three to five

A deliberate deviation, named so it is not read as an oversight. Library and Projects are the only two places nothing else has to hand you. The third job cluster’s screen is Run, and Run is a mode: an entry pointing at it would promise a place nobody can be sent to. A navigation model with an entry that cannot be arrived at is worse than one with two entries.

Depth to the thing the product exists for

The count runs to the archive in hand, which is the furthest point this product owns — it never touches the receiving machine.

PathRouteTaps

Three is the floor, and it is a floor for somebody already signed in. Every path costs one more from a cold, signed-out start; that is the honest thing to say rather than quoting three as though it covered everything.

The two fours are different in kind. C’s fourth tap is a traversal — you started in the wrong place — and it became optional when the corpus arrived inside the builder: the same errand now runs as path B. B’s fourth is a mode entry in the right place, and it is purchasable: a project created a second ago is empty by definition, so entering the configuring mode automatically would put B back at three. The trade is that the empty state is never seen on the path that creates it — and that state is the one surface where somebody learns what a project is for. Four is kept, and what three would cost is on the page.

The depth of this product is in selection, not in traversal, and the count is what makes that arguable rather than a slogan.

No path to the main job passes through the Library, and since 20 Sep that is deliberate rather than a tension. The primary persona is the keeper of a corpus, and their own ground was the one place the product’s central act did not touch. The corpus now reaches assembly as a panel, so the Library does not need to be on the route in order to be present in the work. It is the curator’s room: read, curate, edit, add, delete, usage facts, copy from the shelf, share. If it ever starts growing builder machinery to justify itself, this is the paragraph that explains why.

STEP 04

The flows

Seven paths in eight diagrams. Every node is a screen, mode, overlay, region or state the map already had — no new place appeared in any of them.

That held through a change of mechanism, which is the harder test. The way the corpus reaches the builder was replaced outright — an overlay summoned by a keystroke became a region of the screen — and the place count did not move. What moved was the opposite of what a reader would expect: the main job lost four nodes and four edges, because the panel absorbed an excursion to two other screens and a branch that ended in a wasted archive was removed.

Waits and failures are named in words under each flow and deliberately not drawn. The product is online, so every read and write crosses a network — and putting that in the diagrams would have doubled every graph to say one thing eight times. What ordinary waiting looks like is a composition decision and belongs to phase 04.

Every diagram is a window you can move around in. Drag to pan, Ctrl or ⌘ with the scroll wheel to zoom, Fit for the whole shape and 1:1 to come back — and the keyboard works too, once a frame has focus. The plain scroll wheel is left alone, because a diagram that eats the page scroll is a diagram you cannot get past. The tallest of these is 3,528 pixels at full width; a window is the honest way to show it, and it is why the page does not decide for you whether the shape or the labels matter more.

Two failures belong to every path and are named once. A session that expires mid-work — you return to where you were, nothing is re-run, nothing saved is lost, and you return to the place, not into the act, because you were halfway through assembling a set is not something this architecture can restore and it does not pretend to. And a second tab on the same account: sync means one person can now disagree with themselves. Single user has stopped meaning single writer, and the model has not noticed yet.

STEP 05

Traceability

Every job against every surface. A tick means the surface takes part in closing the job — being a route to the surface that does the work is not participation, or every column would be full and the exercise would return nothing.

Non-screens are columns too. A matrix of screens only would hand Project the tick that belongs to the panel, and Library the one that belongs to the item overlay. The question is which surface does the work, not which of them has an address.

Sourced jobs and hypotheses are kept apart, because a column standing only on the second block is a column standing on an assumption. That is a third kind of defect and the exercise was not asked to look for it.

Orphans highlighted — a column with no tick is a surface no job raises; a row with no tick is a job no surface closes. Both are defects, not “fine”.

Four orphans, and each has a decision

Two empty columns, and they are empty for opposite reasons. JSON, the library import and export, is a mechanism nobody asked for that we chose to keep — it could be cut tomorrow and the product would work. Its warrant is not a job: the product holds somebody’s accumulated corpus on a server and tells them to pour everything into it, and one that offers no way out is the lock-in the word was invented for. In, the sign-in, is a place another decision forced on us. It is not a candidate for removal and never will be, and the two must not be counted as one defect — a forced consequence and an unforced choice look identical in a coverage matrix and have nothing else in common.

Two empty rows, and neither can be honestly filled. SJ-2 — have something I would put my name to — is declined in writing twice: importance 1, established by absence in two instruments, and the catalog is refused. H-J5 — watch what actually ran — is importance 3 with twelve blank cells, and it is the second main-job candidate that survived the method, which by that method’s own rule means two products. The product runs nothing on anybody’s machine, so the screen is forbidden rather than missing. Inventing a tick for either would make the matrix a decoration.

And one row whose ticks do not mean what the others mean. EJ-3 — stop suspecting that half of what I keep is dead weight — is importance 3, and what answers it is usage facts. Those answer is it used. The job asks is it any good. An item can sit in three projects and be useless in all three. The product touches this job and cannot close it, the ceiling is now written beside the facts themselves, and nothing was added to narrow it: counting exports would still be used and would read as useful.

STEP 06

The critique

Sixteen defects in four classes, plus one finding outside them that was larger than any of them. Written first, approved, then applied — and the list was left untouched underneath, because a critique edited after the fact is not a critique.

ClassWhat it foundFixed
Dead endsFour of ten endings did not survive. One flow ended on a problem that cannot be fixed from its row — which contradicts the specification and the diagram beside it, since export is never disabled. Another contradicted itself two branches apart: the same act looped back in one case and ended in the other. One ended where a sibling diagram asks a second question. One ended where the specification supplies a free remedy.6/6
Missing statesThe worst class, and it got worse rather than being discovered. No loading node in 144, and no error node — and the product had just turned out to be online. The sharpest: a receiver opening a revoked link, the one person with no account, no context and no second surface, was undrawn. And one defect was a regression from that morning: removing an excursion took the first-run empty library out of every flow with it.4/6
Excess depthTwo paths at four taps, both already named with their compromise. The critique’s job was to decide whether naming is enough — and to notice that one of the two is purchasable.4/4
OrphansChecked against the matrix, no new matrix built. One column re-justified, two rows disposed of — and then a brand-new orphan class that the matrix had never seen, because the online decision created a door four hours after the matrix was drawn.4/4

The largest finding was outside the four classes

The receiver’s path was not drawn. Their surface carries seven jobs — more than Projects, more than the library panel — and it appeared in the file only as nodes inside the sender’s diagrams. The specification describes that path in prose and even names it — library-to-archive performed by somebody who owns nothing — and nobody had ever walked it. Meanwhile a decision taken the same day commits the product to its most conservative treatment anywhere on exactly those surfaces, because that person has never spoken in the first person in four rounds of research across five venues. A surface we had promised to be most careful with was the one surface with no flow.

Three things the fixes produced that the critique did not predict

The file needed a third kind of ending. Applying is this person actually stuck? moved four endings out of red, and two colours could not hold the difference between you were told and proceeded and there is nothing left to do.

One flow had to be restructured rather than patched, and the restructure is more accurate than what it replaced: nobody knows a requirement is dangling until the walk runs. The product does not ask; the check finds out.

And a measurement rule turned out to be wrong. A diagram fell below the scale floor this work had set itself, so it was rendered on its own and looked at — and every label read without effort, because its width was empty space from two back edges rather than density. The numbers are a screen, not a verdict; the rule is render it and look.

What is open

The lesson is finished. What is not settled is listed rather than smoothed over.

OpenWhat would settle it
Can counts carry a verdict?0 problems · 0 notes reads the same on an empty set and on a perfectly assembled one. The channel may be too narrow for what it is asked to say.
The detached row whose original is deletedIt holds its own overrides. Does it survive, or go with the original? Nothing in the model answers it, and inventing an answer would be worse than recording the gap.
What the shelf holds, and how it sortsDeferred. Its only evidence is provenance over volume, and the shelf gained load three times this phase without gaining a point of evidence.
What the way in actually isPassword, link, provider. The rule was built and the mechanism deliberately was not — how things are served is not decided in this work.
What ordinary waiting looks likePhase 04. The validation pass is specified as a designed moment rather than a spinner, which leaves everything else open.
The matrix owes a re-runWhatever the sign-in surface eventually adds has to go through it, or the coverage claim on this page quietly stops being true.

And one piece of work rather than a question. The public shelf has to be built — around thirty real items from checked sources, each with its origin, a pinned reference and a licence, composed so the example project produces a genuine problem and a genuine note. An information architecture argued against an empty library is argued against nothing. It does not block this phase and it is needed by the next one.

One rule came out of this phase and it is the one most likely to be needed again. Any argument of the form we cannot, because there is no X is only as durable as the absence of X. Two absences ended on a single day — the product had no server, and a score assumed the check lived somewhere it had not lived for weeks — and each had a refusal resting on it. Three were re-argued and survived on other grounds. One did not and was void within hours of being written. When an absence ends, every refusal leaning on it is re-argued or dropped, in writing.
