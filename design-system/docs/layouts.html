Design system / Layouts

Layouts

The frames a screen is set in. A layout holds components and places them; it has no colour of its own beyond the ground. Each frame below is a dressed screen that uses it.

Canvas

.canvascomponents/base.css83 dressed pages

The product's page: 1440 wide, desktop-first (§3), the ground under everything. Every screen sits in one.

Take it

Every screen of the product, once, as the outermost frame.

Take another

A dialog or a popover is not a canvas: it hangs over one, on the Dialog's scrim.

Main column

.app-maincomponents/frame.css83 dressed pages

The column under the App header that holds a place's content: the Page head, then what the place holds.

Take it

Every place with the App header: My library, Public library, Projects, Project.

Take another

Run has its own bar and no App header; a door has its form. Neither takes the main column.

Set with a side panel

.with-drawercomponents/drawer.css6 dressed pages

The set on the left, the item side panel on the right; the row open in the panel carries the pill.

Take it

A project with one of its items opened beside the set: viewing, configuring, a detached row.

Take another

Editing the library original is the Item sheet over My library, not a side panel.

Content with an aside

.with-asidecomponents/aside.css4 dressed pages

The receiver's page: what was shared on the left, and on the right what their machine will need and a way to take it.

Take it

The two receiver surfaces, Shared project and Shared item, and nowhere else (§8, Q20).

Take another

The owner's own places keep the main column; nothing of the owner's is laid out for a receiver.

Configuring layout

.config-layoutcomponents/sidebar.css13 dressed pages

The library panel as a full left sidebar, the set beside it: the configuring mode's frame (Q33).

Take it

The configuring mode of a project, and only there: the panel reads and adds, the set removes.

Take another

Viewing a project is the main column with no panel; keeping items is My library.
