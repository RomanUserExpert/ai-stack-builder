# References — phase 06, stage 1

**Written 2026-10-08.** The course's stage 1: describe the repo first, choose the two screens, then
find how the products our research already trusts look, and take **one base and one or two named
devices from each of the others.** A device is something you can point at: a radius, where a badge
sits, how much colour is on the screen. *"The general feel" is not a device.*

**No new competitors were searched.** Every source below is one the research named in phase 01 as
the **craft benchmark**: Linear · Vercel · Raycast · GitHub · Stripe (`CLAUDE.md` §3, and the plan in
[`README.md`](README.md)).

## 1. The repo, before anything is drawn

**The screens.** [`04-wireframes/pages/`](../04-wireframes/pages/wireframes.html) holds **80 pages**,
each a screen in one state (`library-my-default`, `run-error-check`, …): 17 screens, every state
drawn. The viewer is `wireframes.html`. The map of screens and why each state exists is
[`04-wireframes/_screens.md`](../04-wireframes/_screens.md).

**Is the HTML generated?** **The wireframe pages are not — they are hand-written HTML** (`tools/README.md`).
**What is generated from them** is the 239 clickable prototype pages in `04-wireframes/prototypes/`,
built by `tools/prototypes/build_main.py` and `build_others.py`. The phase pages (`research.html`,
`personas.html`, `ia.html`, `voice.html`) are generated by `tools/build_*.py` from templates. **So
painting a wireframe page repaints its prototypes on the next build** — which is why stage 5 paints
exactly two screens on purpose and stops.

**Shared styles.** One file, [`04-wireframes/pages/wireframe.css`](../04-wireframes/pages/wireframe.css),
737 lines, *"Grey only, Inter, 4 px grid, no colour"*: `--ink #111`, `--ink-2 #555`, `--ink-3 #888`,
`--line #bbb`, `--fill #f2f2f2`, `--paper #fff`, a 1440 px canvas. Every page links it and Inter from
Google Fonts. **10 of the 80 pages also carry inline `style="…"`** — to be found and moved into tokens
in stage 5, not copied.

**`DESIGN.md`.** **There is none, anywhere in the repo.** It is written in phase 07, from built code.
**The leak the course warns about exists anyway, in two places, and neither is the product's
palette:**

- `wireframe.css` — grey on white, light. A model reading it would paint a light, grey product.
- `tools/research-page.tpl.html` — the phase pages' paper ground and orange accent `#e05f03`.
  **That orange is the course site's, and the owner's portfolio uses the same family** (visible in
  our own Vercel capture below). **It is not inherited.** If the product ends up with an accent, it
  comes from an attribute in stage 2, not from this file.

### A · Linear, dark — the base

**Why it is the base.** It is the persona's daily tool (§3) and the only reference that is already a
**dense list of named things with state**, which is what `My library` is. And it already agrees with
rules this product wrote before it looked: colour is reserved for meaning, hierarchy is carried by
weight and dimming. *We take its system, not its surface* — no violet, no Linear iconography.

**The full reading** (from our 1:1 captures, `NOTES-linear.md`):

- **Surfaces: three.** Page ground near-black; one raised surface a few percent lighter; one selected
  fill lighter again. **The sidebar shares the page ground** and is separated by **one hairline**, not
  a tonal step.
- **Elevation by border, not shadow.** The command palette's lift is a hairline plus a whisper of
  tone; the shadow is barely there.
- **Text: three tones.** Primary near-white for titles; secondary for identifiers, dates, counts;
  colour only where it means something.
- **Group headers carry a dimmed count beside the label** — `In Progress 4`, `Todo 4`, `Done 17`.
- **Labels are grey chips with a single coloured dot** — `● Research`, `● UX`. The colour lives in
  ~6 px; the chip itself is neutral.
- **Key caps** as a component — `C`, `N then P`.
- **Empty properties are verbs** — *Set priority*, *Add label*, never `Priority: —`.
- **Density:** single-line rows, ~11 rows per group visible, title and metadata on one line.
  *(Pixel row height is not read from a downscaled capture; it is set in stage 4 on the 4 px grid.)*

### B · Vercel / Geist — devices

**Device B1 — a stage row is name left, duration and a verdict glyph right, a chevron to expand.**
`vercel-deployment-stages.jpg`: `› Build Logs ……… 32s ✓` · `› Deployment Checks ……… ◷`. The verdict
is a **glyph at the far right edge**, the duration sits immediately left of it, and the row expands in
place.
*Where:* every stage of `Run` (`01 Requirements … 0.3 s`).
*The worry it removes:* **"did it actually look, and at what?"** — a duration next to every verdict is
evidence of examination, which is principle 1 of `voice.md` (*say what was examined*) turned into
layout. A verdict without a duration is a claim; with one, it is a report.

**Device B2 — status is a small dot plus a word, never a filled pill.** `● Ready`, `● Error` in the
deployment list and the details header (`refs/lazyweb-vercel-deployments-list.jpg`). The dot carries
the colour; the word carries the meaning; the row stays quiet.
*Where:* the `Run` head (`Checked just now · 1 problem · 2 notes · 1 skipped`) and the verdict on each
`Projects` card.
*The worry it removes:* **"is this a badge telling me the thing is good?"** — a filled green pill reads
as a seal; a dot and the word *Checked* read as a fact. §5 refuses badges; this is how a status
appears without becoming one.

*Also recorded from Geist, for stage 4 rather than taken now:* named **materials with stated radii** —
`material-base` and `-small` **6 px**, `-medium` and `-large` **12 px** (`geist-materials.png`). Two
radii, chosen by elevation, not by component. A candidate for our radius scale; the decision is
stage 4's.

### C · GitHub dark, Actions — devices

**Device C1 — `Skipped` is its own neutral glyph: a circle with a slash, in the secondary grey.**
`github-actions-failed-job-steps.png`: *Configure git to use LF endings* and *Install Rust* wear `⊘`,
beside steps that wear `✓`. Same size, same weight, no colour.
*Where:* `03 Command names — Skipped`, and every `Skipped` in `Run` and its counts.
*The worry it removes:* **"is that green tick earned?"** — §6 asks for exactly this, *not a green tick
it did not earn, and not a red one it does not deserve*, and GitHub is the one benchmark product that
already draws it. Taken whole.

**Device C2 — the count sits in a small grey capsule right after the label.** `Issues 1.3k`,
`Pull requests 2.2k`, `Security and quality 61`.
*Where:* the kind tabs in `My library` (`All 11 · skill 1 · agent 2 · …`) and the library panel tabs.
*The worry it removes:* **"how much do I have of each, before I click?"** — principle 4, *counts and
names, not adjectives*. It also tells the collector at a glance where the dead weight might be.

*Not taken from GitHub:* the bright green check-in-a-disc on passed jobs. A filled green disc is the
*works* seal §6 refuses; our *Checked* is a quieter mark (B2).

### D · Raycast Store — one device

**Device D1 — a dimmed metadata line under the description, with a small icon before each fact.**
Each extension card ends in `author · ◎ 9 Commands · ⊙ 359,272` in the secondary tone, and the one
action (`Install`) sits **top-right of the card**, on the title line.
*Where:* the `My library` row's last line — `Used in 3 projects · 1 item requires this · last exported
12 days ago`, and for public items `@ 9be4674 · MIT`.
*The worry it removes:* **"is this used, and where did it come from?"** — the collector's suspicion
that half of what they keep is dead weight (§5's ceiling), and the licence question for anything that
is not theirs. The facts are present on every row and quiet enough not to compete with the name.

*Not taken from Raycast:* its red brand ribbons, the large app icons and the centred marketing
layout. They are a store's, and a library is not a store.

**None is copied whole.** Linear gives the system and nothing of its brand; Vercel, GitHub and Raycast
give two, two and one named devices. **What was explicitly refused** is listed under each — a filled
green disc, a filled status pill, brand red ribbons, store-sized icons — because each one claims more
than this product knows, or belongs to a different kind of product.

**What this file does not decide.** No colour value, no font, no radius is chosen here — those come
from stage 2's attributes and the owner's taste, and are fixed in stage 4's `tokens.css`. This file
says **where to look and what to take**; Geist's 6 / 12 px is recorded as a candidate, not adopted.
