Already decided.
  • Material: Mica — a neutral ground with a faint lime tint, quiet layers; acrylic only around the Export mode and a dialog, text always on an opaque sheet.
  • Type: Schibsted Grotesk; Source Code Pro for paths, refs and file contents. Icons: Lucide, 1.75 px stroke.
  • Brand: lime #C6EF5A, only on what you can press and where you are. Density: between Default and Comfortable.
  • Rows: each item in its own rounded cut-out, auto-added items inside their parent’s behind a dashed rule. No rings round a row for a finding or a selection.
  • Checked is a quiet dot, never green (D1-B).
  • Navigation: the top bar (N1). Active row: a short lime pill at its left edge, the cut-out one step lighter (S2). Findings: a tinted tag with the icon and the word (F1). Current place in the bar: a raised tile, its icon in lime; the other places sit on nothing (T2, no track).
