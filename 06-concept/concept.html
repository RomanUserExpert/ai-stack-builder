Colour

Each colour with the attribute or decision it follows from (concept.md, Attributes and Working style).

Primary — lime, the one colour that acts

Lime is the brand and the only colour that invites an action: the primary button, where you are, the active row. It never states a verdict, so it can never be read as “works”.

  • --lime #C6EF5Athe brand: what you can press, where you areOwner, round 4 (Lime); pair 5: colour is a claim, lime never a verdict
  • --lime-hover #D3F57Athe primary button, hoveredStates
  • --on-lime #141A08text on limeContrast
  • --lime-mark #C6EF5Alime as a mark: the current place’s icon, the active row’s pillSame as lime in the dark; its own value in light

States — what was found

Problem and Note carry colour because they state something the product knows. There is no success colour: the best a check says is Checked, a quiet dot. The red is kept for an act that removes something.

  • --problem #FF8F98Problem — the archive will be wrong§6: three severities; pair 1
  • --note #F2C661Note — correct but incomplete§6: three severities
  • --checked #C5C7BEChecked — a quiet dot, no colour: the product has no successD1-B: never a green tick that claims works
  • --skipped #A1A49ASkipped — a slashed circle, neutral§6: not a green tick, not a red one
  • --danger #D4363Fa button that removes somethingOwner, 2026-10-11: a red remove
  • --danger-hover #BE2E37the remove, hovered — darker, never lighterContrast: white stays above 4.5:1

Form — radius, spacing, shadow

Soft but not round: 6 px on controls, 10 on cut-outs, 14 on panels, 18 on the acrylic frame. Everything on a 4 px grid. Edges are hairlines; a shadow appears only where something is lifted or pressed.
