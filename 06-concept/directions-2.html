Three more directions for AI Stack Builder

Phase 06, stage 3, round 2. Round 1 — Acrylic, Manifest, Catalogue — was turned down, and none of its directions comes back here. These three are new worlds, and they are allowed to change the layout: the wireframes fixed what is on the screen, not how it looks. Each one rearranges the Project screen its own way — a left rail and stems, a drawer of cut-outs, soft cards and a rising sheet.

What did not change: every word is from microcopy.md; dark-first (CLAUDE.md §3); colour only where it states something; no score, no badge, no green tick that claims works. Between them the three still cover D1 and D2–D4.

This grey page is not part of any direction. Ratios on the swatches are WCAG contrast, computed when the page is built.

Direction 1 of 3

Glasshouse

A palm house at night. The work grows on a deep bottle-green ground; what you enter — the Export mode, a dialog — is a pane of glass in a thin brass frame, and the dependencies that pulled an item in are drawn as the stem it hangs from.

Victorian glasshouses: wrought-iron glazing bars, bottle-green glass, brass fittings, the serif of a botanical label beside plain working type.

  • Taste Teams / Slack glass, noticeable but sparing — here it is literally framed, never under text
  • Pair 4 glass is a layer over the work: the set and the item panel are opaque, only modes are panes
  • Research “serif for ideas, sans for the product” — Linear’s own method page (refs/README)
  • Pair 5 a green ground makes green mean nothing, so no green tick can claim works
  • Layout a left rail for the three places; the set without boxes; auto-added items hang on a curved stem
  • Glass frame only: a brass-framed pane, the findings on an opaque sheet inside it (D2, D3)
  • Checked A — no colour; on a green ground green cannot be the verdict (D1)
  • Icons Fluent regular, 20

Guessable from the subject? Not from the subject: nothing about AI tools says bottle green and brass.

Honest risk. The most atmospheric of the three. A whole green product is a commitment people either keep or tire of, and the serif title has to stay on titles — on a row it would read as decoration.

Kit

The drawer of a well-kept tool chest. Every item sits in its own soft cut-out in dark foam, and the foam has a coloured layer underneath: colour only shows where something is wrong or missing. A step that had nothing to check is an empty cut-out.

Two-tone shadow foam in a mechanic’s drawer: a black top layer, red or yellow beneath, rounded outlines cut to the shape of each tool, steel on top.

  • Research the practitioner keeps it in a folder called ~/dev/kit — “40-something files” (interviews.md:376-378)
  • EJ-3 “maybe half of it” does nothing: a drawer shows at a glance what is used and what is missing
  • Pair 5 colour is a claim — here it is literally underneath, and only shows through a gap
  • Pair 2 density held by the outlines and the type, not by colour or cards
  • Layout a top bar; each item in a rounded well; auto-added items sit inside their parent’s well
  • Glass none — foam and steel are opaque, the dialog is a lid with a real shadow (D4)
  • Checked B — a quiet steel dot, not green (D1)
  • Icons Fluent filled, 20

Guessable from the subject? Not from the subject: charcoal foam and steel belong to a workshop, not to AI.

Honest risk. Inset wells are a physical metaphor; pushed too far they become skeuomorphic. The wells must stay quiet enough that fifty of them still read as a list.

Direction 3 of 3

Warm

A tool that feels made by a person for a person. A warm espresso ground, soft cards with generous corners, comfortable type, and one bright colour that only ever appears on something you can press.

The best consumer apps: soft-cornered cards on a warm tinted ground, plain-language labels, a single saturated action colour, sheets that rise from the bottom.

  • Owner “beautiful, convenient, not so square” — the brief for this round
  • Pair 3 the consequence sits inside the card, above the button, never in a toast
  • Voice plain words, counts and names — the copy already reads like a person wrote it
  • Pair 5 the one bright colour is an action, never a verdict, so it cannot be read as works
  • Layout pill navigation; each item a card, auto-added items inside it; Export rises as a sheet
  • Glass a soft frosted veil behind the sheet; every sheet and card is opaque (D2 variant)
  • Checked A — no colour. C is not drawn in this round: two of three directions use green or near-green for the room or the key, where a green Checked would read as the button (D1)
  • Icons Fluent regular, 20

Guessable from the subject? Not from the subject: warm brown and lime are a consumer palette, not an AI one.

Honest risk. The furthest from the Linear / Vercel craft bar the research assumes (H11, a hypothesis). Soft cards cost density; at fifty items the cards must stay one line tall or the collector scrolls forever. Lime must never appear on anything that is not pressable.
