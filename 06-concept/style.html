START

Overview

Seven stages in a week, four rounds of directions, and the two lists they produced.

What we saw

  • The category has a default, and it is easy to land in. A near-black ground with violet-blue neon, glass cards, icons in circles — the look an AI tool gets by default. The owner’s anti-taste refused it in the first stage, and every direction was checked against it: could this palette be guessed from the subject alone?
  • The research already named the craft bar. Linear, Vercel, Raycast, GitHub — read for how they show a verdict, a state and a count, not for their brands. Linear became the base: three surfaces, hairlines instead of shadows, colour only for meaning.
  • Colour is a claim, exactly as a word is. The product never claims works, so it has no success colour: Checked is a quiet dot. A green tick would have said what the product cannot know.
  • The owner chose by looking, not by reading. Round 1 and round 2 were turned down whole; round 3 kept one style and one density; round 4 kept one colour; then a dozen precise corrections on the screens — a pill, a tag, a segmented track, a muted remove — each written down with its reason.
  • Measuring caught what the eye did not, and the eye caught what measuring could not. The script found a hover that dropped below AA and lime vanishing on white; the owner found a hover gone dark because a token resolved in the wrong theme.

What we decided

  • One style, Mica · Lime. A neutral ground with a faint lime tint, quiet layers, Geist and Geist Mono, Lucide icons, a 4 px grid, density between default and comfortable.
  • Lime only where you can act or where you are — the primary button, the current place, the active row. Never a verdict.
  • Findings are tinted tags, Problem and Note; red only removes; amber only goes ahead despite a Problem. No rings round rows.
  • Glass only around a mode or a dialog, text always on an opaque sheet — the owner’s signature, used sparingly.
  • Every value lives in tokens.css, and no page holds one of its own — a build refuses it.
  • The wireframes stay. The main job is drawn as design twins of the wireframe pages, one switch away.

STAGE 01

References

Where to look, and what to take — one base, and one or two named devices from each of the others.

No new competitors were searched: the sources are the craft benchmark the research named in phase 01. Linear is the base — a dense list of named things with state, colour reserved for meaning. From Vercel, a stage row with its duration and verdict at the right edge, and status as a dot and a word. From GitHub, a neutral slashed circle for Skipped, and a count in a capsule after a tab. From Raycast, a dimmed line of facts under a description. None is copied whole, and each device is written down with the worry of the persona it removes.

Refero was not connected; Lazyweb, SaaSUI and the captures from phase 01 were used instead, and the file says so. A wider idea base of 61 images was captured for the later stages.

STAGE 02

Taste and attributes

The owner’s taste in named products, and five pairs of opposites drawn from the data.

Likes: Vercel — status, fact and consequence side by side, monochrome; Figma — a dense surface held by the grid and the type, not by colour; Teams and Slack — glass, noticeable but sparing. Does not want: the palette an AI produces for “AI tool”, a marketplace showcase, glass everywhere, a green tick as the main signal.

Five attributes, each with the line of research it comes from: an inspector’s report, not a seller’s badge; dense and counted, not spacious and showcased; the consequence visible before the step; glass is a layer over the work, not the work; colour is a claim, held back. Five conflicts between taste and data were not resolved on paper — they were drawn as variants and chosen in the browser.

STAGE 04

The stand

The style at work, then every value with its reason — colour, type, form, icons, three components, a light slice and the contrast table.

Every value lives in tokens.css — 143 custom properties, generated by tools/concept_tokens.py, each colour with a comment naming the attribute or decision it follows from. Contrast is measured by a script on the file as written: 86 text-on-surface pairs in dark and in the light slice, all AA. The light slice did its job — it found that lime as a mark disappears on white, and light got its own --lime-mark.

STAGE 05–06

Mockups — the main job

The style proved on the screens the product exists for: Projects, Project and Run, the contrasting one — every state of each.

The wireframes were kept. Instead of repainting them, tools/build_mockups.py writes a design twin of each wireframe page — the same markup, the same words — with a stylesheet that takes every value from tokens.css, Lucide icons for the grey placeholders and the portrait for the avatar. 22 pages and the 7 steps of the flow main-success are drawn; the viewer, renamed Wireframes and mockups, switches any of them between Wireframe and Design.

Projects ↗ Project ↗ Run ↗ The flow main-success ↗

A critique by two independent reviewers scored the first pass 29/40 and found a broken icon, a colour that contradicted its own count, and a flat payoff at the end of the export. All three were fixed in the stylesheet and the generator, never in the words.

STAGE 07

The check

Every page in the style, held against concept.md — 29 problems, each with its evidence, and an audit.

Two measuring passes computed the contrast of every text node, compared every repeated component within a page, and listed every literal value; /impeccable audit scored 13/20 (accessibility 3, performance 3, theming 3, responsive 1 — phase 10 — integrity 3). The owner decided four: a warning colour for Export with 1 problem, accessible names for + Add, no hidden heading or skip link yet, and Add items from the left for the empty project. And one more, in passing: Export lost its ellipsis everywhere — Q38, over Q36.

The rest was fixed in one order — tokens, then the generator, then the stand and the screens: focus that no longer reaches behind an open dialog, a spinner that stops for reduced motion, durations and measures and skeleton sizes taken from tokens, tones set by class rather than by context, dead links sent to their wireframes, sprites cut to what each page uses, the portrait stored in the repository.
