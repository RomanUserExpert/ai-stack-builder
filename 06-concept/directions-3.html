Three calmer directions, at three densities

Phase 06, stage 3, round 3, after round 1 and round 2. What you asked for: calmer but better made; Acrylic and Kit were the closest; the type and the spacing were too large. So two directions are calm descendants of those two, the third is the category standard done properly, and density is its own switch — choose a style and a density separately, and combine them.

The samples are drawn at 1:1 on a 1280 px canvas, never scaled up. Every word is from microcopy.md. Ratios are WCAG contrast, computed at build.
