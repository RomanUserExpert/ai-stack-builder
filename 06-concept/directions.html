Three directions for AI Stack Builder

Phase 06, stage 3. All three are dark-first (CLAUDE.md §3), and they differ on the other three axes at once: how much colour (Catalogue almost none, Acrylic a tinted ground and one accent, Manifest a committed ground and a paper surface), the character of the type (Segoe’s humanist, Archivo’s widths, Red Hat’s neutral grotesk) and the form (4 / 8 px and acrylic, 2 px and ruling, 0 px and hairlines).

The sample is fixed (README, stage 3): the Project screen with eslint-autofix open, then the Export mode over the project with the remove dialog over it. Every word is from project-item.html, run-remove.html and microcopy.md, unchanged. Between them the three cover D1 (A, B and C for Checked) and D2–D4 (glass at the frame, glass as the scrim, no glass).

This grey page is not part of any direction. Each direction is one block, top to bottom: what it is, its palette, type, icons and parts, then the sample at full width. Ratios on the swatches are WCAG contrast against the direction’s own ground, computed when the page is built.

Direction 1 of 3

Acrylic

The work is opaque and still. What you enter is acrylic laid over it — the Export mode, a dialog, a menu — so the place you came from shows through, blurred, at the edge.

Microsoft’s Fluent: Mica under the app, acrylic on what is temporary, Segoe’s optical sizes, 4 px controls and 8 px flyouts.

  • Taste Teams / Slack glass, “noticeable, but sparing”; Fluent icons are the same family
  • Pair 4 glass is a layer over the work, never the work: rows, the item panel and the findings stay opaque
  • Pair 2 dense rows held by type weight and a tinted ground, not by cards
  • Glass frame and edges only; text always on an opaque inner sheet (D2, D3)
  • Checked B — a quiet steel dot, not green (D1)
  • Icons Fluent regular, 20

Guessable from the subject? Not from the subject: an “AI tool” guess is near-black with violet-blue glow. Graphite-blue Mica with a flat berry, no gradient, no glow.

Honest risk. Graphite with one accent is the nearest of the three to the dark-tool cluster; the berry has to stay flat and rare, or it slides toward the violet the taste refuses. Segoe is a system face: on a Mac it falls back to SF.

Kept from the challengers this direction was weighed against
  • Severity is carried by shape as well as colour — a crossed circle, an i, a slashed circle, a tick — so it survives greyscale. Kept from a dance-notation score, where shading and shape encode level without colour.
  • Every count and duration is set in tabular figures in one right-aligned column: 0.3 s and 0.1 s read as data. Kept from a data-sublime field of monospace numerics.
  • One family through the whole scale: Segoe’s Display size for the title, Text for the rows. Kept from a type specimen that repeats one face at five sizes.

Direction 2 of 3

Manifest

A project is a consignment. Every row is a declared line, every fact sits in a labelled field, and the item you open is the paper declaration pulled out of the hall — the one light surface in a dark product.

Customs and shipping papers: the manifest, the declaration form, the inspection slip; petrol-dark container steel, kraft paper, stamp inks.

  • Pair 3 the consequence sits in a labelled field before the step, like a declared value
  • Pair 1 an inspector’s slip, not a seal: what was examined, when, the counts
  • Data the export is a handover — the archive leaves for a receiving machine (§6)
  • Glass only as the scrim: the place behind a mode is frosted, every sheet is opaque (D2 variant)
  • Checked C — small green, never the largest thing on screen (D1)
  • Icons Fluent filled, 20

Guessable from the subject? Not from the subject: petrol and paper belong to freight, not to AI. Nothing violet, nothing glowing.

Honest risk. The most committed of the three, and the paper panel is a light surface inside a dark product: it has to earn that by being the place a person reads closely, and it doubles the severity colours. A document world can turn into a costume if the ruling spreads past the fields.

Direction 3 of 3

Catalogue

Matte black and white hairlines. Nothing is coloured until it states something — so on this screen one Problem and two Notes are the only colour, and a check is drawn as lines whose length is the time each stage took.

A record label’s catalogue sleeve: matte black, a white hairline plot as the body ink, small engraved caps, vast unprinted ground, colour only where a code is decoded.

  • Taste Vercel: monochrome, colour only for an error or a warning; Figma: the grid and the type hold the hierarchy
  • Pair 5 colour is a claim, held back — strictest reading
  • Pair 1 a duration on every verdict, drawn: the report shows it looked
  • Glass none — every surface opaque, a dialog sits on a black scrim (D4)
  • Checked A — no colour: a tick in the secondary grey, as quiet as Skipped (D1)
  • Icons Fluent regular, 20

Guessable from the subject? Not from the subject, but from the category: black and white is the dev-tool standard. Kept because the owner named it, and because D1-A and D4 need a real alternative.

Honest risk. The closest to the category’s craft standard: black, white and one red is how Vercel already looks. What is ours is the plot and the discipline, not the palette — and a grey screen with one colour can read as unfinished.
